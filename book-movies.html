A Life in Brief

A few milestones from a life that carried him across four continents.

1937

Harbin, China

Born in September to Russian émigré parents, an only child.

1937–49

Shanghai

Grew up in the French Concession and was educated at École Ste. Jeanne d'Arc.

1949

Berkeley, California

Emigrated to the U.S. as a teenager, the first in his family to attend college.

1955–60

UC Berkeley

Studied architecture and met his first wife, Maria Mothershead.

1960–64

Chapel Hill, NC

Earned the University of North Carolina planning department's first PhD.

1964–70

UC Berkeley Faculty

Rose from lecturer to full professor in six years.

1970–75

Northwestern University

Built a new urban systems engineering and policy planning program.

1975–83

Vienna, Austria

Led the Human Settlements and Services research area at IIASA.

1983

Boulder, Colorado

Joined the University of Colorado, founding its Population Program.

2000

Walter Isard Award

Received the Award for Distinguished Lifetime Scholarly Achievement.

2008

Retirement

Retired from full-time teaching after 25 years at CU Boulder.

2018

Mindel C. Sheps Award

Honored by the Population Association of America.

2026

Portland, Maine

Passed away peacefully on August 18, at age 88.

In loving memory of Andrei Rogers · September 1937 – August 18, 2026 ♥
